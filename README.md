## Some mythology

Pallas is the daughter of Triton (himself a son of Poseidon). She is described as a warrior and a childhood friend of Athena. During a festival, Pallas and Athena fought in a friendly mock battle to determine the best warrior. Pallas was taking advantage over Athena, but Zeus, refusing to see his goddess daughter being defeated, decided to distract Pallas with the Aegis (an artifact carried in Greek mythology by both Zeus and Athena). Pallas was indeed distracted and did not dodge Athena's fatal attack. In remembrance of her friend, Athena made a Palladium, a wooden statue that she placed near Zeus.

source: Apollodorus, Bibliotheca (https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Apollodore_-_Biblioth%C3%A8que_(%C3%A9d._Clavier),_vol._1.djvu/388)
